    Hold W as the countdown hits 1 for a boost start. Drive close behind a rival to slipstream. Hold Space through a corner and let go for a mini-turbo. Press F to use an item.

    Track Maker

    A small 3D editor that shows the road exactly as the race will build it. Left-click a point to select it and drag to move it along the ground. G grabs the selected point (then X or Z locks an axis, click or Enter confirms, Esc cancels). E extrudes a new point, A adds one on the selected segment, double-click the ground to drop one, and X or Delete removes it. H turns the road leaving the selected point into a highway: wider, raised on a deck with ramps at each end (cyan line). Right-drag orbits, Shift plus right-drag pans, the wheel zooms, and 1 3 7 jump to front, side and top views. The green S is the start line and the cars head toward point 2. Highways cannot cross other roads. Y grabs the selected point and locks it to the vertical (height) axis: move the mouse up or down, or use the Point height slider. The road climbs smoothly between points, so keep height changes gradual. Ctrl+Z undoes.
