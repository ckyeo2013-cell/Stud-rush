      Track Maker

      A small 3D editor that shows the road exactly as the race will build it. Left-click a point to select it and drag to move it along the ground. G grabs the selected point (then X or Z locks an axis, click or Enter confirms, Esc cancels). E extrudes a new point, A adds one on the selected segment, double-click the ground to drop one, and X or Delete removes it. H turns the road leaving the selected point into a highway: wider, raised on a deck with ramps at each end (cyan line). Right-drag orbits, Shift plus right-drag pans, the wheel zooms, and 1 3 7 jump to front, side and top views. The green S is the start line and the cars head toward point 2. Highways are bridges: they stand on pillars, so they can pass over other roads (use Medium or High height and give the bridge a flat stretch before the crossing). Y grabs the selected point and locks it to the vertical (height) axis: move the mouse up or down, or use the Point height slider. The road climbs smoothly between points, so keep height changes gradual. Ctrl+Z undoes.

      Backup & transfer save

      Your trophies, owned and equipped parts, best times, settings and My Track live in this browser only. Save a backup file or a code, then load it on another device or in another version of the game. Loading merges: whichever save has more races wins the trophy total, purchased parts and best times are combined.
